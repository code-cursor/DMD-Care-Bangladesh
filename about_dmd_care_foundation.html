About DMD Care Foundation Bangladesh

A non-political, non-profit, and humanitarian service organization dedicated to the welfare of children affected by Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) and their families.

"Hope, Care, Cure"

"আশা, সেবা, আরোগ্যের পথে একসাথে।"

Empowering Lives, Spreading Hope

Since its founding, the organization has been working tirelessly to raise public awareness about DMD, promote timely diagnosis, expand advanced treatment and rehabilitation, develop research and knowledge, and provide comprehensive support to affected children and their families.

The core mission of DMD Care Foundation Bangladesh is to ensure a dignified, safe, and promising life for every child affected by DMD, and to empower their families through information, guidance, treatment, rehabilitation, and emotional support. We believe that a child's treatment is not confined within the four walls of a hospital; rather, genuine improvement in quality of life is possible only through the combined efforts of family, society, education, rehabilitation, awareness, and empathy.

Grounded in the values of empathy, humanity, transparency, accountability, and service based on scientific knowledge.

We are determined to build a Bangladesh where no child affected by DMD is left behind due to neglect, lack of awareness, or absence of treatment — where every child has the opportunity to develop to their fullest potential, and every family receives the necessary support, dignity, and humanitarian assistance.

Our commitment: to raise awareness, spread accurate information, ensure timely diagnosis, expand advanced treatment and rehabilitation, empower families, encourage research, and work hand in hand with partners at home and abroad.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build an inclusive, humane, and compassionate Bangladesh, where every child, adolescent, youth, and adult affected by Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy has access to timely and accurate diagnosis, quality treatment, rehabilitation, education, necessary support, and opportunities for social inclusion. We aspire to build a society where every affected individual can live with dignity, respect, equal rights, and the assurance of a promising future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to work dedicatedly toward improving the quality of life of individuals affected by Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy and their families. We are committed to building a strong, sustainable, and humane support system through raising awareness, facilitating early and accurate diagnosis, providing reliable information on treatment and rehabilitation, empowering families, encouraging research and innovation, establishing patients' rights, and coordinating with partners at home and abroad.

Our Objectives

To increase scientific and accurate, information-based awareness about Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy among the general public, physicians, teachers, educational institutions, media, and policymakers.

To provide necessary information, guidance, and support for genetic testing to ensure early diagnosis of affected children.

To provide reliable information, emotional support, care guidelines, and necessary counseling for patients and their families.

To establish and maintain a reliable National Patient Registry for individuals affected by Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy in Bangladesh.

To increase information and support regarding treatment, physiotherapy, vocational rehabilitation, respiratory and cardiac care, and multidisciplinary treatment approaches.

To work jointly with national and international research institutions, medical experts, and universities on research, innovation, and knowledge exchange.

To play an effective role in incorporating the rights and necessary services of individuals affected by Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy into government policy, healthcare, and social protection programs.

To work toward ensuring equal opportunities in education, employment, social participation, and dignified living.

To work toward the long-term establishment of a modern DMD service, research, and information center in Bangladesh.

  • Showing equal respect to all people regardless of religion, race, gender, language, age, region, or financial status.

Organizational Policies

As a humanitarian organization reliant on the trust of our donors and beneficiaries, we adhere strictly to the following policies.

The best interests of patients and families will be given the highest priority in every decision and activity.
The safety, dignity, rights, and best interests of every child will be ensured.
The personal information of patients and families will be securely maintained, and no information will be disclosed without proper consent.
Full transparency, proper record-keeping, and regular auditing will be ensured for all donations, funds, and expenditures.
No discrimination will be made on the basis of religion, race, gender, language, region, disability, or financial status.
All members, officers, staff, and volunteers will maintain honesty, professionalism, and ethical conduct.
Work with national and international partners will be carried out on the basis of mutual respect, trust, transparency, and accountability.
Scientific research, innovation, and evidence-based treatment initiatives will be encouraged and supported.
All members and volunteers will follow the Foundation's constitution, values, policies, and code of conduct.
The safety and protection of patients, children, families, members, and volunteers will be ensured in every activity of the Foundation.
